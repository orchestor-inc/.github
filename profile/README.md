[Orchestor](https://www.orchestor.io) helps marketing teams understand how their brands appear in AI answers and decide what to improve. Use the web app, work from your terminal, or bring Orchestor into your agent through skills and MCP.

### From questions to action

- **Measure your visibility.** Monitor questions that matter to your customers and compare your brand with competitors.
- **Follow the evidence.** Inspect saved AI answers, cited pages, and the sources behind a report.
- **Find your next move.** Research content gaps and turn findings into proposals and tracked tasks.
- **Work in context.** Keep brands, questions, reports, and actions in the right workspace.

### Start with your agent

Add the skills to your agent and select the ones you need:

```sh
npx skills@latest add orchestor-inc/skills
```

Or install the plugin bundle:

```sh
npx plugins add orchestor-inc/orchestor-plugin
```

Choose one installation route for the same skills. Follow the [CLI setup guide](https://orchestor.io/docs/cli) to connect your workspace; CLI and MCP authentication are separate.

Then ask your agent:

> Compare our brand's visibility with these competitors over the past 30 days. Show the cited sources and suggest the next three actions.
